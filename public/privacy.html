Roopip Calendar privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026.

Roopip Calendar is a private tool that the Roopip team uses to plan and publish Roopip's own Instagram and Facebook posts. Only people the Roopip team has invited can sign in.

What the app uses from Google

When someone connects Google Drive, the app asks Google for permission to see the files in the Roopip media folders and to save files it creates (such as edited reels and Canva exports) to Drive. It uses this only to show, suggest and attach photos and videos to Roopip's planned posts.

The app keeps a Google sign-in token on Roopip's own Cloudflare account so the team does not have to sign in on every visit. It also stores small preview details (file names, folder names and colour summaries) to suggest suitable photos. It does not store copies of your Drive files beyond the photos and videos it uploads for a scheduled post, and those are deleted within three days.

What we don't do

We don't sell, share or use Google data for advertising, and we don't transfer it to anyone except as needed to publish Roopip's own posts to Instagram and Facebook. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Click "Sign out of Drive" in the app to delete the stored sign-in, or remove "Roopip Calendar" at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
