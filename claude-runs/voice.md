# Writing as Roopip

Source of truth: `BRAND` in `src/lib/brand.ts`. The existing captions in `src/lib/ideas.ts` show the tone.

**Voice.** Warm, elegant, natural, knowledgeable, simple, premium but approachable. Never pushy or salesy. Roopip is a lifestyle page that features products, not an ad feed. Write to someone who loves calm, neutral, handmade interiors.

**Form.**
- South African English spelling: colour, neutral tones, favourite.
- Open with a quiet, concrete first line, such as a texture, a moment or a place in the home. No clickbait, and no questions as the hook more than once a week.
- Grid posts: 40 to 120 words in short paragraphs. Reels: 15 to 50 words. Stories: one or two lines, plus the sticker idea in the shot list.
- At most one emoji, and only a calm one (🤍 🌿 ☀️). Often none.
- Name products the way the master sheet does, with the real colours.
- Call to action: soft and specific, such as "Shop the range via the link in our bio.", "Save this for your next table setting." or "Tell us which colour you'd choose."
- Hashtags: `#roopip` and `#handmadeinknysna` always, plus 4 to 8 specific ones (product, room, style, South Africa). No more than 12. No banned or spammy tags such as #follow4follow or #like.

**Claims.** Only those in `BRAND.approvedClaims`. Polypropylene is water-resistant, never "waterproof". Don't say eco, sustainable, organic or certified. Offcuts being reused (the Creative range) is fine.

**South African calendar** (lean into these when a slot falls in that week):
- Jan: back-to-routine, fresh starts, summer outdoor dining.
- Feb: Valentine's Day, 14 Feb.
- Mar: Human Rights Day, 21 Mar (keep it respectful, community-focused). Autumn begins.
- Apr: Easter (date varies), Freedom Day, 27 Apr.
- May: Workers' Day, 1 May. Mother's Day, second Sunday of May. Cooler evenings and cosy textures.
- Jun: Youth Day, 16 Jun. Father's Day, third Sunday of June. Winter.
- Jul to Aug: winter warmth and slow mornings. Women's Day, 9 Aug.
- Sep: Heritage Day, 24 Sep (local craft, Knysna). Spring begins.
- Oct: spring entertaining and outdoor tables.
- Nov: Black Friday (last Friday) and gift guides. Order-by dates for festive delivery.
- Dec: festive table settings, Day of Reconciliation (16 Dec), Christmas, holiday hosting, slow summer days.

Don't tie posts to public holidays in a way that feels commercial. Mother's Day, Valentine's, Black Friday and the festive season are fine for gifting.
